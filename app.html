      What the paper covers

      This is independent revision material, not an official or leaked paper — no university publishes its entry maths test in advance. Four things here are known rather than guessed: the test runs 1 hour 30 minutes, it is sat online, the pass mark is 40%, and the place is on a foundation-year programme, which is the level these tests are written for. The questions themselves are mine, so the wording will differ from the real paper even where the topics match. One thing still worth confirming with the admissions office: whether a calculator is allowed, and if so whether it is your own or one built into the screen. The 60% pass mark used on the test here is deliberately stricter than the university’s 40% — it is a margin, not the real condition.

        Exam conditions

        The test runs for 1 hour 30 and submits itself when the clock reaches zero. Two things make it feel like the real thing, and you choose both.

        Your camera, on screen

        Your own face appears in the corner while you work, the way an invigilator would be in the room. Nothing is recorded, saved, or sent anywhere — the picture exists only on this screen and disappears the moment the test ends. Your browser will ask permission, and you can refuse.

        Leaving the screen is noted

        If you switch tab or click away from the test, the time away is counted and appears in your report. Nothing stops you doing it — but in the real test you could not, so the record tells you honestly how you worked.
